Trendelenburg Gait

Primary Etiology:

Gluteus medius weakness / superior gluteal nerve lesion

Kinematic Deviations:

Pelvis drops on contralateral unsupported side during stance phase.

Compensatory Mechanism:

Lateral trunk lean toward the affected stance limb (compensated).
