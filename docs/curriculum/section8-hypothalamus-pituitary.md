# Hypothalamus & Pituitary

### 1. Introduction
This chapter covers the physiological principles of Hypothalamus & Pituitary.

### 2. Daily Life Analogy
The hypothalamic-pituitary axis is like a military command chain: the general (Hypothalamus) issues local orders (releasing hormones) to the colonel (Anterior Pituitary), who sends field officers (trophic hormones) through the blood to direct the soldiers (target glands: thyroid, adrenal) to perform operations.

### 3. Basic Concept
The hypothalamus-pituitary axis is the master command system of endocrinology, integrating neural inputs to regulate growth, metabolism, reproduction, and stress.

### 4. Anatomy Review
Hypothalamus, Median Eminence, Hypophyseal Portal Vessels (linking to Anterior Pituitary/Adenohypophysis), and the Hypothalamic-Hypophyseal tract (direct axonal connection to Posterior Pituitary/Neurohypophysis).

### 5. Physiology
Anterior pituitary hormones: TSH, ACTH, LH, FSH, GH, Prolactin. Posterior pituitary hormones (synthesized in hypothalamus, stored in posterior pituitary): ADH (vasopressin) and Oxytocin.

### 6. Mechanism
Feedback loops: Target gland hormones (e.g. Thyroxine) exert negative feedback by binding receptors on anterior pituitary thyrotropes (inhibiting TSH) and hypothalamic neurons (inhibiting TRH) to maintain homeostasis.

### 10. Clinical Correlation
Pituitary adenomas can cause mass effect (compressing the optic chiasm, causing bitemporal hemianopsia) or hypersecretion syndromes (Prolactinoma, Acromegaly).

### 11. Disorders
1. Prolactinoma: Most common secreting pituitary tumor, causing galactorrhea and amenorrhea. 2. Sheehan Syndrome: Postpartum pituitary necrosis due to blood loss during delivery.

### 15. Viva Questions
1. What is the hypophyseal portal system? **Answer**: A system of blood vessels connecting the hypothalamus with the anterior pituitary, allowing rapid, undiluted transport of releasing hormones.
2. Where are ADH and Oxytocin synthesized? **Answer**: In the supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei of the hypothalamus.

### 16. MCQs
1. Which of the following hormones is synthesized in the hypothalamus and stored in the posterior pituitary?
   * A) Growth Hormone
   * B) Prolactin
   * C) Oxytocin
   * D) TSH
   *Answer*: C (Oxytocin and ADH are synthesized in the hypothalamus and transported axially to the posterior pituitary for storage and release.)

### 17. Case-Based Learning
A patient presents with double vision and loss of peripheral vision in both eyes. MRI reveals a 1.5 cm pituitary mass. **Analysis**: The pituitary tumor (macroadenoma) is compressing the overlying optic chiasm, causing bitemporal hemianopsia (mass effect).
